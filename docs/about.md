# About this portal

This is the University of Colorado Cancer Center (UCCC) instance of
[cBioPortal for Cancer Genomics](https://www.cbioportal.org/). It's maintained by the UCCC
Associate Director for Informatics and Data Science.

**Contact:** [seandavi@gmail.com](mailto:seandavi@gmail.com)

## What's here

- **Every public study on cbioportal.org.** They're loaded from cBioPortal's weekly public database
  export, currently the dump of 2026-10-18, so studies added to cbioportal.org since then aren't
  here yet.
- **Public cohorts added for UCCC disease groups.** To request a cohort, email the contact above.
